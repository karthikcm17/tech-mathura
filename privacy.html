This Privacy Policy explains how Tech Mathura, a brand operated by Mathura Universe (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores and protects personal data when you visit this website or contact us. It is written to meet our obligations under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (together, the “DPDP law”), and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it, as applicable from time to time.

02Who we are and what this policy covers

Tech Mathura is the web and Android app development brand of Mathura Universe, based in Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu, India. For personal data collected through this website, Mathura Universe is the Data Fiduciary: it decides why and how that data is processed.

This policy applies to this website, our enquiry forms and the conversations you start with us by email, phone or WhatsApp. It does not apply to websites or apps we build for clients (see section 8), or to third-party websites we link to, such as our product sites, which have their own policies.

07When we share your data

We never sell, rent or trade your personal data. We share it only:

  • with the service providers listed in section 6, only as needed to provide their service;
  • with team members or contracted professionals working on your project, who are bound by confidentiality;
  • with government authorities, courts or law-enforcement agencies when required by law or a valid legal order; and
  • with a successor entity if Mathura Universe is restructured, merged or acquired, in which case this policy will continue to protect your data.

08Websites and apps we build for clients

We design and develop websites, web applications and Android and iOS apps using a wide range of technologies. These include frontend frameworks (such as React, Next.js, Angular and Vue), backend platforms (such as Node.js, Django, Laravel and Spring Boot), databases (such as PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB and Firebase), cloud services (such as AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure and Vercel), payment gateways (such as Razorpay and Stripe), and AI services (such as OpenAI and Google Gemini).

When we build or maintain a product for a client:

  • the client is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data of its own users, and is responsible for its own privacy policy, user consents and legal compliance;
  • where we handle that data, we act only as a Data Processor on the client’s documented instructions and under a written agreement;
  • we apply reasonable security safeguards (secure authentication, encryption in transit, access control, and backups where in scope), and we do not use client users’ data for our own purposes; and
  • third-party services chosen for a project, such as cloud hosting, payment gateways, analytics or AI providers, process data under their own terms, which the client accepts.

On request, we can advise clients on privacy-by-design and help them implement consent, notice and data-deletion features, but this is not legal advice.

09How we protect and store your data

We use reasonable security safeguards to protect your personal data, including:

  • HTTPS encryption across the website;
  • enquiry data kept in a private Google account protected by two-step verification, with access limited to people who need it;
  • spam protection on our forms; and
  • regular review of who has access.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will act promptly to contain it and will inform affected individuals and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.
