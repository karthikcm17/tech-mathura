Native or cross-platform?

Two ways to build.One honest recommendation.

We explain the trade-offs in plain language and recommend what fits your users, budget and timeline.

Native AndroidKotlin + Jetpack Compose
  • Best speed and battery life
  • Full access to Android features
  • Ideal for Android-first audiences in India
Cross-platformFlutter or React Native
  • One codebase for Android and iPhone
  • Lower cost, faster launch
  • Ideal when you need both stores
